# Malaysia — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Kuala Lumpur

### Air Quality

As of late October 2026, KL's air quality has deteriorated badly: AQI readings of **149–200+**, low visibility, and effectively no sun for weeks. Locals describe it as the "new Chiang Mai" — meaning roughly six months of the year are now considered unlivable on air alone.

### KL vs Bangkok (as a Long-Term Base)

Community comparison from someone living in both: Bangkok has **worse sidewalks, worse street-level smog, and more noise** than KL. On balance, KL remains the preferred long-term base despite the air quality problem — the baseline livability outside of burn periods still beats Bangkok's day-to-day friction.
