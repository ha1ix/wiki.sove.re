# Portugal — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Madeira

### Getting Around

A **rental car is essentially mandatory** if you plan to leave Funchal. The island's terrain is steep enough that public transit won't reach the places people come to see — without a car, you'll miss most of the island's actual draw.

### Food

Local cuisine centers on **seafood, fish, meat, and pastries**. Vegetarian and vegan options are improving, especially in Funchal. A good main dish runs **€8–25**, averaging around **€15 including a drink**.

### Beaches

Madeira has almost **no natural beaches** — the exception is a small black-sand beach in **Seixal**. If beach access is a priority, the **Canaries** are a better match. For nature and views instead, the **southwest between Calheta and Ponta do Pargo** is the recommended stretch.
