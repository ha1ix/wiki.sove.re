# Taiwan — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Visa & Tax

### Digital Nomad Visa — Updated Limits (October 2026)

Taiwan has extended its digital nomad visa to allow stays of up to **2 years**: **6 months initial + three 3-month extensions**. Previously capped at 1 year. For nomads who want to actually settle rather than cycle through, this materially changes Taiwan's position as a longer-term base.

## Accommodation

Airbnb inventory is limited in Taiwan. Hotels frequently have **no windows** or are overpriced for what you get. For shorter stays, **Agoda** is the community-mentioned decent option — not perfect, but a better hit rate than Airbnb for finding usable rooms.
