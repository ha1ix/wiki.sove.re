# Israel — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Tel Aviv

### Cost of Living

A comfortable one-bedroom with **safe room access** runs around **$3.5k USD/month** (3-month data point, late 2026). The city is expensive by any measure, but produce quality is reported as excellent — a non-trivial factor for anyone cooking at home.

## Entry

For a one-month tourist stay, having an **onward ticket** is strongly advised — border officials have been asking for one, especially in the current climate. A cheap flight to Athens is the community-standard throwaway if you don't have real onward plans.

## Ecosystem Notes

Israel's startup ecosystem leads regional comparisons on **successful startups per year, GDP contribution, and unicorns**. Dominant sectors: cybersecurity, AI, deep-tech, defense. Useful signal for anyone evaluating Tel Aviv as a work base rather than just a stop.
