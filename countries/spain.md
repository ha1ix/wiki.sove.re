# Spain — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Barcelona

### Rentals

**Badi** — room-finding app with a Tinder-style mutual-like mechanic. Reported (October 2026) as less scammy than Facebook groups for finding rooms. Budget tier lands around **€600/month**; the app also lists higher-end apartments but the community recommendation is specifically for the budget band.

**Muppy** — comparable to Badi in quality for short/medium-term stays, reported as slightly cheaper. Rooms are often decently renovated but priced above typical long-term local rentals.

**Why short/medium rentals cost more**: short- and medium-term rental operators in Spain face fewer regulations than long-term lettings and target outsiders willing to pay for flexibility. Roommates in these units often arrive with service-quality expectations the price point doesn't actually deliver — calibrate accordingly.

**Backup channel**: the "digital nomads Barcelona" WhatsApp chat is reported as a reasonable fallback when Badi doesn't yield matches.

## Madrid

### Local Recommendations

**Runroom** (studio/agency that hosts events in the city) surfaces as a channel for local recommendations when planning a stay — their event listings pull in people who actually know the city.
