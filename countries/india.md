# India — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Bengaluru (Bangalore)

Not representative of most Indian cities — **newer, more developed, and more transplants** than typical. Community comparison frames it as closer to Seattle or Silicon Valley in character than to the rest of India: lots of nerdy twentysomethings, tech-anchored. If you expect "India" and land here, you'll find something different; if you want a soft entry to the country, that mismatch cuts in your favor.
