# Panama — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Residency

Panama residency is an active topic in the community. Named service providers that come up when nomads are shopping firms:

- **The Jerz group**
- **The Rothbard group**

These are the two most commonly compared when looking for a residency firm. No consensus verdict from the community — worth getting quotes from both and asking recent clients about turnaround and hidden fees rather than relying on brand alone.

Some smaller boutique firms also offer bundled residency + banking + incorporation packages, which can be worth pricing against the larger groups if you need more than just the residency filing.
