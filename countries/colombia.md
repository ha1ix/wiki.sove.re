# Colombia — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Medellín

### Neighborhoods

Community preference (October 2026) leans toward **Laureles** over Poblado for a quieter stay. Window insulation is thin across the city — avoid units directly on bar streets if noise matters.

The **Estadio–Laureles–Belén Rosales** corridor is reported as walkable, green, and secure, with good local commerce.

Differences within the corridor:
- **Laureles** — cleanest of the three.
- **Estadio** — some stretches have river smells; occasional addicts around.
- **Belén** — airport flight paths make orientation of the apartment matter; wrong side can be noisy.

### Rentals

Direct landlord deals in Estadio side-streets have surfaced as cheaper than Airbnb for medium-term stays — flexibility depends on the specific building/owner, not advertised on platforms. Worth asking around locally rather than booking blind.
