## Mugla Region

### Bodrum

Known for nightlife scene. Gets notably crowded and expensive in August — plan around it. Climate is less humid than Antalya but still hot in summer.

### Akbük

Suggested as a quieter alternative to Bodrum in the same region — worth considering for those who want the coast without the crowds.
