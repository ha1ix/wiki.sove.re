# China — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Shenzhen

### Practical

- Affordable coastal apartments within ~25 minutes of Shenzhen by high-speed rail run roughly **$150–350/month**. These are not listed on major platforms like Trip.com; finding them requires direct contact with local agencies or owners.
- Foreigners renting these units face friction: **police registration** requirements (landlords often unwilling or unaware how to handle) and the absence of a vetted review platform equivalent to Airbnb, so unit quality and landlord reliability are hard to verify in advance.

### Makerspaces & Coworking

- **Chaihuo** makerspace: ~¥1500/month for 3D printers, soldering stations, and desk space. Primarily oriented toward hardware projects — fine as a desk, better if you actually have something to build.

## Hangzhou

Mentioned in community discussion (October 2026) as a reasonable base — road cycling scene active enough that nomads coordinate rides locally.

## Coworking (National)

### WeWork

Current WeWork mainland locations (October 2026): Beijing, Chengdu, Guangzhou, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Suzhou, Wuhan, Xi'an.

Open community question: after WeWork China was sold to a local firm, whether a non-Chinese WeWork Plus membership still grants access to these locations is unclear. Verify before relying on it for workspace continuity.

### Beeplus

Reported day rate: **35 RMB/day including drinks**.

## Visa & Entry

### 30-Day Visa-Free Border Runs

Reported working (October 2026) on the 30-day visa-free entry: a same-day border run from Shenzhen into **Hong Kong** and back is routine. **Macau** and **Xiamen** work the same way. No overnight required.
