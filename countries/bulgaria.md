# Bulgaria — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Bansko

Established nomad hub with recurring community presence. One of the most frequently mentioned long-stay destinations in nomad circles, alongside Cape Town (South Africa).

## Plovdiv

Pleasant small city with a unique historical atmosphere. Community verdict: not worth building a trip around, but if you're already going somewhere in Bulgaria, it's likely the best choice.

## Sofia

Nice enough but not particularly engaging on a week-long stay. Reasonable comparison point: **Tbilisi** — both are medium-sized, moderately cheap cities set in mountains. If you've done one, there's a case for picking the other for variety rather than doubling up.
