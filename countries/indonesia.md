# Indonesia — Nomad Notes

*Compiled from community digests.*

## Bali — Denpasar

As a base, Denpasar is **cheaper and away from the tourist clusters** (Seminyak, Kuta, Canggu) — a workable choice if the goal is to save money while still being on the island. Nightlife is passable if you're into clubs and have a local contact who can get you into Indonesian venues, but it's not the draw. The tradeoff is the usual one: less of the "Bali expat" scene, more of the actual city.
