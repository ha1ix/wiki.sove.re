## Payments & Banking

### Crypto → PIX / FX Wrappers

The same crypto-backed PIX wrappers used in Brazil also work in Argentina:

- **Peanut** and **Yodl** — both operate across Brazil, Argentina, and parts of Asia. Yodl sometimes has better FX rates on larger payments.
- **Belo** — separately cited as a cross-border option specifically between Argentina and Brazil. Residency requirements were an open question in the community; verify before relying on it if you're not resident in either country.

**comparapix.ar** — community-shared site (Argentine domain) for comparing live PIX-to-crypto and FX rates across providers before sending.
